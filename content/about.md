Hi, I'm Mathias, a Brazilian computer scientist with a PhD in mobile robotics, currently living in Stuttgart, Germany. I work at [Sereact](https://sereact.ai), where I'm part of the team developing and deploying AI-powered robotic systems for industrial environments — working with manipulators, humanoid robots, and large-scale warehouse automation.

My background is deeply rooted in mobile robotics. During my years at the [Phi Robotics Research Lab](https://www.inf.ufrgs.br/phi-group/site/) at UFRGS, I worked on SLAM, autonomous navigation, object search, and robot exploration. After completing my PhD, I moved to industry — first building autonomous ground robots at [Instor](https://instor.com.br/en/) in Brazil, and then making the move to Europe to work at the intersection of robotics and modern AI.

These days, my focus has shifted toward what I see as the next big frontier: embodied AI and Physical AI. I work daily with the challenges of making large language models and foundation models actually run on real robots — from deployment and system integration to building AI systems that understand and act in the physical world. Industrial robotics, robotic manipulation, and humanoid robots are the domains where I spend most of my time.

My passion for learning has not slowed down since my PhD days. The robotics field is moving faster than ever, and there is nothing more exciting than being right in the middle of it.

As the world is not just about robotics ~~unfortunately~~, I also like to spend my time listening to podcasts, talking to friends, and watching videos on YouTube.
